Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026. This policy must be checked against the actual services and data practices used by the website before publication.

1. Introduction

Parklane ("we", "our" or "us") aims to handle personal information responsibly. This policy explains how information may be collected and used when you browse listings, create an account or submit a property enquiry. Where required by law, we will seek consent separately.

2. Information we may collect

Depending on the features you use, information may include your name, email address, phone number, account details, property preferences, preferred locations, budget range and details submitted through contact, valuation, buying, renting or selling forms. Website hosting or enabled analytics services may also process technical information such as browser type, device details, pages visited and diagnostic logs.

3. How information may be used

  • Respond to property enquiries and requests for information.
  • Discuss property preferences, viewings and valuation requests.
  • Operate accounts and website features you request.
  • Maintain security, troubleshoot issues and improve the website.
  • Measure website performance where analytics are enabled.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations and address disputes.

4. Sharing information

Relevant information may be shared with service providers that host the website, process forms, deliver communications, support analytics or maintain security. Information may be shared with a relevant advisor or service partner when needed to respond to your request, or disclosed when required by law. The actual recipients depend on the services configured on this website.

5. Cookies and analytics

The website or third-party tools may use cookies or similar technologies for essential functionality, preferences or analytics if those tools are enabled. Update this section to name the tools actually used and explain any consent choices offered.

6. Retention and security

Information should be retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the stated purpose, unless a longer period is required or permitted by law. Reasonable safeguards should be used to protect information, but no online system can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your choices and requests

Subject to applicable law, you may be able to request access to, correction of or deletion of your information, or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Contact Parklane using the verified contact details published on the website. Some information may need to be retained for legal purposes.

8. Third-party links

External websites and services operate under their own privacy policies. Review those policies before sharing information with them.

9. Children's privacy

If you believe a child has provided information in circumstances where this is not permitted, contact the website operator so the matter can be reviewed under applicable law.

10. Changes

This policy may be updated as the website, services or legal requirements change. The revised version will be published on this page.

11. Contact

For privacy requests, use the verified contact details on the Contact page. Add a monitored privacy email and the legal identity/address of the website operator before launch.

Important: This is a general draft, not legal advice. Confirm actual data flows, form handling, hosting, analytics, retention and applicable Indian privacy requirements with a qualified professional before publishing.
